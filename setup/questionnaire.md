# Setup questionnaire

Instruction for the agent: Ask one group at a time. Write each answer into its target file. Remove the `TODO(setup)` line. Show the finished file.

## Group 1 — Style → `_config/style-guide.md`
1. Tone: faithful to the Japanese, natural English, or playful? (Example: natural English)
2. Name order for characters: Japanese (Surname Given) or Western (Given Surname)? (Example: Western)
3. Honorifics: drop, keep, or keep only some? (Example: drop)
4. Swearing and mature content: keep the strength, soften, or skip? (Example: keep)
5. Is the setting real history (Sengoku Japan) or original sci-fi/fantasy? (Example: sci-fi mix, use made-up names as is)

## Group 2 — Tools → `_config/tools.md`
6. Is RetroArch installed? What is the path or app name? (Example: /Applications/RetroArch.app)
7. Do you also have a debugger emulator such as Mesen or bsnes? (Example: no, install Mesen)
8. What is the file name of your ROM? (Example: game.sfc)

## Group 3 — Credits and release → `_config/legal.md`
9. Credit name for the patch notes. (Example: "Translated by Zhad")
10. Patch name and version. (Example: MyGame-EN v0.1)
11. Short message for the title screen or notes, if any. (Example: none)

## Group 4 — Glossary seed → `_config/glossary.md`
12. Do you know any official or fan English names for the game, characters, or places? List them. (Example: the official English title of the game)
13. Any names you want kept in Japanese form? (Example: place names)

## Group 5 — Play-testing → `stages/05_playtest/input/bug-notes.md`
14. Do you speak or read some Japanese to help check meaning? (Example: a little kana)
15. How do you want to note bugs? (Example: screenshot file name plus one line)

Line limits are not asked here. Stage 02 finds them from the game.
